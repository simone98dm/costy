Costy 💰

Track your subscriptions with elegance and intelligence

A beautiful and intuitive Flutter app for managing your recurring expenses. Stay on top of your subscriptions with smart reminders and detailed insights.

View on GitHub Explore Features

✨ Features

📊

Interactive Dashboard

View all your active subscriptions at a glance with a beautiful donut chart. Get instant insights into your spending patterns.

🔔

Smart Reminders

Never miss a renewal date. Get timely notifications before your subscriptions are due for payment.

📅

Comprehensive History

Track both active and inactive subscriptions with powerful filtering options. Keep a complete record of all your expenses.

💳

Flexible Billing Cycles

Support for daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly subscriptions. Track any recurring payment with ease.

🎨

Beautiful Design

Smooth animations and a modern dark theme interface crafted for the best user experience.

💾

Privacy First

All your data is stored securely on your device. No cloud sync, no data sharing - your privacy is protected.
